# Development Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Playable foundation

- Mobile-first 3D vertical slice
- San Diego-inspired district
- Walking, driving, NPC interaction, first mission
- Business launch and educational decision loop
- Local save and economy HUD

## Milestone 2 — Player identity and city loop

- High-quality animated male/female character system
- Urban luxury clothing and SUM GREATNESS cosmetics
- Expanded Gaslamp, waterfront, neighborhoods, and traffic
- NPC schedules, dialogue trees, jobs, and business contacts
- Garages, vehicle ownership, properties, and rentals

## Milestone 3 — Business simulation

- Streetwear, food, creative, service, real-estate, and tech businesses
- Staffing, inventory, pricing, marketing, expenses, taxes, and cash flow
- Practical lessons tied to missions and consequences
- Achievements, leaderboards, guild/community systems

## Milestone 4 — Mobile services and monetization

- Capacitor/native packaging for iOS and Android
- RevenueCat entitlements and optional VIP pass ($4.99–$9.99/month)
- AdMob rewarded-main, rewarded-interstitial mission, shop-banner, and app-open placements
- Cosmetics, premium vehicles, homes, clothing, and optional bundles
- Non-pay-to-win economy validation

## Milestone 5 — Production 3D and launch

- Optimized animated characters and San Diego assets
- Audio, accessibility, device performance tiers, and cloud saves
- Multiplayer/community validation
- App Store and Google Play compliance, testing, analytics, and release

## Product constraints

- No violence, weapons, or criminal gameplay
- Progress must remain possible without spending money
- Business education is part of core progression, not a separate textbook mode
- Visual direction is premium urban luxury: dark materials, gold accents, expressive animated characters
